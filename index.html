:blobcatgamer: Switch Switch 2 to CRT.

Have you ever imagined what the Nintendo Switch would look like if Nintendo had produced it in the mid-1990s? [Joel Creates] evidently did, because that’s exactly what this retro CRT-toting Switch 2 dock looks like.

⁉️And yes, it is portable, thanks to a 100W power bank torn apart and built into the 3D printed case.⁉️

https://youtu.be/wcym2tHiWT4

👾However, the idea of recessing handles inside the case so it could be thick enough but still comfortable to hold was all base-model H.Sap brainpower. There are shoulder controls hidden in those recesses, too, for the games that can use them.👾
